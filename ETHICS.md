# Responsible use

Declawd is an educational inspection and carrier-cleaning project. Use it on content you own or are authorised to modify. Review each selected Unicode edit in context because joiners, controls, variation selectors and spaces can carry meaning.

## Decisions

Findings and scores must not support claims that text is human-written, that someone used AI or that a production watermark was present or removed. Do not use them for employment, education, disciplinary, legal, forensic or authorship decisions.

The CLI's scope is its listed registry, selected text edits and embedded PNG/JPEG C2PA stores. It supplies no automated rewriting, synonym selection, broad Unicode normalisation, in-place mutation or recursive bulk cleaning.

## Teaching scores

The SynthID lab accepts token-ID traces from a fixed public reference. It supplies no production key, detector threshold or arbitrary-text verdict. Its exact means describe that contract and cannot establish a claim about a person or piece of prose.

The website's word-pair checker and registered text experiments use their own published profiles. Read each [record's results and limits](docs/EVIDENCE.md), including misses, false positives and reused material, before drawing a conclusion.
