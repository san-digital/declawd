# Responsible use

Declawd is an educational inspection and carrier-cleaning project. Use it only
on content you own or are authorised to modify.

It must not be used to claim that text is human-written, that a person used AI,
or that a production watermark was present or removed. Do not use its findings
for employment, education, disciplinary, legal, forensic or authorship
decisions. A listed Unicode character may be essential language or rendering
data. Review every selected change in its linguistic context.

The project deliberately does not provide automated rewriting, synonym
selection, broad Unicode normalisation, in-place mutation or recursive bulk
cleaning.

The SynthID laboratory accepts token-ID traces only. It exposes no detector
threshold, production key, arbitrary-text checker or authorship verdict. Its
fixed profile is an educational reproduction of a public reference and is not
compatible with Anthropic's production watermark. Do not convert its exact
mean scores into an allegation about a person or a piece of prose.
